## Lab Summary

In this lab, I will be configuring trunks. Trunk ports are used to send VLAN information between switches while carrying traffic from multiple VLANs over a single physical link. I will be configuring trunk ports on each switches then verifying trunking is enabled.

## Reflection Questions

### 1. What did you do in this lab?
For this lab, I configured trunk ports by enabling trunking on interface G0/1 and G0/2 on switch 1. Then I set VLAN 99 as the native VLAN. After that, I resolved the native VLAN mismatch on switches 2 and 3. Finally, I verified the trunk configuration on both switch 2 and 3 to ensure everthing was set up correctly. 

### 2. What did you learn?

I learn the important role trunk ports play in managing VLANs across a multi-switch network. For example, trunk ports allow VLAN traffic to pass between switches, ensuring that devices in the same VLAN can communicate, even if they are connected to different switches.
